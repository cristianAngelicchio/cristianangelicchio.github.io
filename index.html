FPS Multiplayer Level

Train Station

Team Deathmatch map built in UEFN from sketch to its dressing.

RoleLevel Designer
EngineUEFN
TeamSolo
GenreFPS - Team Deathmatch
Duration1 month
Year2026
Gameplay reel (20–25 s)uefn-loop.mp4

Goal

A war-torn train station that could exist in real life. The map is long and narrow, so fights keep moving between the platforms and the tracks, like a train in motion.

What I did

  • Set the goal and sketched the layout on paper.
  • Built and iterated the greybox in UEFN.
  • Tested and tuned sightlines, spawn safety and readability.
  • Dressed and lit the final map.
