Train Station

A Team Deathmatch map set in a war-torn train station.

A 6v6 Team Deathmatch map built in UEFN, set in a realistic train station. Players have to keep moving around the level and fight over unique weapons and timed objectives. Each area is designed to play and look different.

Goal

A war-torn train station that could exist in real life. The map is long and narrow, so fights keep moving between the platforms and the tracks, like a train in motion.

Failed version

First version sketchuefn-v1-sketch.jpg

The first version was symmetrical: each team had its own base, with two areas between them, the platforms and the station lobby. It never felt right, and it failed on three things I needed from the map:

Stagnant gameplay

Fights stayed on one area at a time, and the middle of the map became the focus.

No decision-making

The active objective dictated the optimal playstyle too harshly.

Visually similar

Both halves of the map risked feeling the same, making players get lost.

For these and other reasons, I redesigned the map around the train wagons, the most interesting part of this first draft.

Techniques

Sightlines & cover

Controlling how far players can see and shoot, so long-range weapons don't own every space.

Landmarks

Each area has something that stands out, so players recognize where they are.

Affordance

Visual cues that tell players where they can go.

Looking back

What worked

Playtests showed the map works: players found their way easily, and each area felt different to fight in.

What I'd change

Better lighting, so no area feels too dark and paths are clearly lit, and fewer routes where too many paths lead to the same place.
